About Debt Collection & Our Platform

Kanyakla Debts streamlines financial recovery by providing the agency with an intuitive interface to manage customer balances, record manual cash transactions instantly, and trigger automated M-Pesa STK Push prompts directly to debtors' phones.

Our platform is designed to enhance efficiency, reduce manual errors, and ensure timely collections. With real-time tracking and comprehensive reporting, the agency can make informed decisions and maintain healthy cash flow.
